# Interactive Skills Enhancer (ISE)

**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2024 – Finalist** 
---

## Project Overview

**Interactive Skills Enhancer (ISE)** is a **Virtual Reality–based education system** designed to improve **social and interpersonal skills** in children with **Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)** and **Intellectual Disabilities (ID)**.

Children with special developmental needs often face challenges in traditional classroom environments due to sensory sensitivities and difficulties in social interaction. ISE addresses this gap by providing **safe, immersive, and customizable VR environments** where children can practice real-life social scenarios at their own pace.

The platform focuses on **learning through experience**, allowing children to repeatedly interact with realistic social situations such as classrooms and playgrounds, helping them understand emotions, communication cues, and appropriate social responses.

---

## Key Features

* **Immersive VR Social Scenarios**

  * Classroom interactions
  * Playground communication
  * Daily-life social routines

* **Personalized Learning**

  * Adjustable difficulty levels
  * Sensory-friendly customization options

* **Monitoring Dashboard**

  * Tracks child progress and interaction patterns
  * Enables parents and educators to tailor learning plans

* **Safe & Repeatable Practice**

  * Encourages learning through repetition
  * Reduces anxiety in real-world social settings

---

## Expected Outcome

* Improve **social interaction skills**
* Better **emotional recognition and response**
* Increased confidence in real-life social environments
* Data-driven insights for educators and parents

ISE aims to enhance the **quality of life** and **learning effectiveness** of children with ASD and ID through innovative, immersive technology.
